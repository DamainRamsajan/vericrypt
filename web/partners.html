◈ The Only Compliance Tool Where the Rules Are Open, Auditable, and Provable

Every other PQC tool on the market is a black box. The compliance rules are hidden in proprietary code. Banks, regulators, and legal counsel cannot audit how regulations are encoded, cannot verify correctness, and cannot customise the rules to their jurisdiction.

VeriCrypt is different. The regulatory axiom library is open ASL source code — compiled by seedc, executed deterministically by seedvm, producing a verifiable schedule trace. Banks can use the standard axioms encoding DORA, NIST FIPS 203/204/205, NCSC, and PQFIF requirements. Or they can edit the axioms to reflect their jurisdiction, internal policy, or supervisory expectations — recompile, re-run, and produce a signed .pqc report proved against the exact rules they chose. Regulators can publish their own axiom packs for all banks in their jurisdiction to use.

Mode 1: Standard axiom library · Mode 2: Bank-customised axioms · Mode 3: Regulator-specified axioms. No competitor can offer Mode 2 or Mode 3. This is a genuine and durable competitive moat.
